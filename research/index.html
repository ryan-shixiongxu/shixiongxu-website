Research

I am interested in how legitimacy, reputation, credibility, and trust shape organizational behavior and decision-making, particularly in international organizations and global governance. My research brings together perspectives from organizational studies and international relations.

My Latest Research

Self-Legitimation Through Naming and Shaming: Evidence from the UN High Commissioner for Human Rights, 2006–2026

This project examines how UN human rights officials use public naming and shaming to respond to legitimacy challenges facing the Human Rights Council, and how these statements can function as institutional self-legitimation.
