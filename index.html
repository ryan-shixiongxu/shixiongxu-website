Hey! Amazing You

I am Ryan (Shixiong) Xu, a recent graduate of the University of Chicago’s Committee on International Relations (CIR), the oldest graduate program in international relations in the United States.

My research focuses on international organizations, bureaucratic autonomy, and legitimacy in global governance, with particular interests in international peace and security, human rights, and global health governance.

Methodologically, I am interested in computational text analysis, machine learning, and statistical methods, including regression analysis, time-series analysis, and causal inference. My research is also deeply informed by critical theoretical perspectives, particularly feminist and queer theory.

Beyond academia, I love doing stand-up comedy, translating literature (especially the works of French writer Marguerite Duras), baking, dancing to electronic dance music (EDM), and spending time in nature.
